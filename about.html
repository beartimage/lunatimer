About

Calmer time, on purpose

lunatimer is a small collection of beautifully simple timers built around one idea: different parts of your day deserve different kinds of attention.

Most of us try to run our whole day on a single stopwatch. But a quiet morning meditation, a deep-focus work block, and a long list of small tasks each ask something different of us. lunatimer brings together three purpose-built timers — Meditation, Pomodoro, and Timebox — so you always have the right tool for the moment, without the clutter of a dozen apps.

What we care about

  • Calm over noise. A warm, paper-like interface and soft, synthesized sounds — designed to lower the temperature, not raise it.
  • Focus over features. Each timer does one job well. No dashboards, no streak-guilt, no endless settings.
  • Respect for your privacy. Your presets and settings live on your device. There's no account to create and nothing to sell.
  • Works anywhere. lunatimer runs in any modern browser and installs to your home screen, working fully offline once loaded.

The three timers

Meditation Timer — a calm dial with gentle Tibetan-bowl chimes and optional interval bells, for meditation, breathwork, or a mindful pause.

Pomodoro — focus sprints and breaks that cycle automatically, keeping you productive without burning out.

Timebox — a time-blocked task list that helps you move through the day one task at a time.

What's next

We're always refining lunatimer with care. Optional accounts and sync are on the way, so your presets can follow you across devices — while everything that makes lunatimer calm and private stays exactly as it is.

Have an idea or some feedback? We'd genuinely love to hear it. Reach us any time on the contact page.
